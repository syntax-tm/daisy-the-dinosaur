Marley,

I don’t know if you will still remember me by the time you get to read this book. I had hoped to be able to read this book to you myself during one of your weekly visits. Unfortunately, I don't think that I will ever get the chance. My plan is to ask your beautiful, smart, and amazing mom for one last favor if she would be willing to read this book to you.

I want you to know that you truly are an amazing kid. I’m sure that a lot of people say the same thing but, I never understood why anyone would ever want kids until I met you. There’s no doubt in my mind that you’re going to do great things in life as you grow up. I loved how passionate you are about so many different, especially creative, things. You have the same desire to learn about things that I've always had. My hope is that whoever will be a part of your life as you grow up will help continue to encourage your love of learning.

No matter what anyone ever tries to tell you, you've got the best mom anyone could ever ask for. She loves you more than you'll ever know and there’s nothing that she wouldn’t do for you. Please don’t ever forget that, not even for a second. One of the most meaningful parts of my life was the time I spent with you and your mom. I consider myself lucky to have been able to be a part of your life, and I’m sorry I won’t be able to be a part of it going forward.

Hopefully you enjoy this book. I am sorry that it took so long, but I promise you that it’s something that I have been thinking about and working on since the day you first told your mom you wanted me to make you a "dinosaur book."

Your Best Friend,
Trey
