When Daisy the Dino has to spend some time away from home, she faces big feelings, new surroundings, and a lot of uncertainty. With the help of a kind blue bird, caring grandparents, and new friends who welcome her with open arms, Daisy discovers that being away doesn't mean she has been forgotten or forgotten.

Through play, friendship, and a little courage, Daisy learns that it's okay to miss home, it's okay to feel scared, and most importantly, love can find you wherever you are. When the day finally comes for Daisy to return home, she carries something special with her—a heart full of gratitude for the people who cared for her along the way.

A gentle story about foster care, change, resilience, and the reassuring truth that a child is loved wherever they may be.
